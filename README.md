This is a GTM addon mod for Minecraft modpack CTNH.
This mod adds a new multiblock structure, which can consume energy to create and maintain a portal to another dimension.

Thank LuckyBlock and other CTNH-team members, they help me with the codes a lot.
Thank Moonpools very much, she offers the texture of portal frame and controller.
